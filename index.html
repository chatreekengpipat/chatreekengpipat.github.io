01 · SAP FI Tax

SAP FI Tax, upstream to downstream

Accenture · S/4HANA FI-Tax · how each WRICEF hands off to the next

Tax is a chain, not a single program. Set-up decides what an invoice posts; postings feed the daily and month-end adjustment programs; one reporting engine turns them into certificates, attachments and e-filing files; the returns are due on fixed dates. Play a thread to follow one document end to end, or click any box to see what feeds it, what it feeds — and what breaks if it fails. Object codes are neutral (anonymized), the same as in the FI-Tax Knowledge Lab.

  1. Set up — tax procedure, tax codes & accounts · extended WHT types & codes · business partner WHT data · segment derivation · three local currencies.
  2. Post — vendor invoice (FB60 / MIRO) · payment with WHT (F110) · customer billing & receipt · joint-venture cash calls.
  3. Adjust — WHT FX adjustment (10:00 batch) · deferred input / output VAT transfers · VAT36 self-assessment after month-end.
  4. Report — one WHT reporting engine · VAT report with net VAT clearing · Oman VAT report · Myanmar WHT report.
  5. Output — 50 Tawi & PND54 form · PND3 / 53 / 54 attachment · RD e-filing text files · PND54 ↔ VAT36 reconciliation · PP36 report · PP30 file.
  6. File & hand over — PND3 / 53 / 54 and PP36 by the 7th (e-filing 15th) · PP30 by the 15th (e-filing 23rd) · certificates to payees.

01 · SAP FI Tax

Try the mechanics

Four simulators · standard SAP behaviour · sample numbers

Change a number, press play, and watch the documents, the postings and the ledger move. Nothing here is a client system — standard SAP S/4HANA behaviour and Thai tax rules with sample amounts.

Animated document flow for four processes — Procure-to-Pay, Order-to-Cash, Thai withholding tax at payment, and a foreign service with PND54 and VAT self-assessment. Each step shows the T-code, the tables written and the debit/credit posting, while a T-account ledger fills up and clears.

Thai withholding tax and VAT on a payment: pick who you pay and for what, and see the WHT rate, the form (PND3 / PND53 / PND54), the 50 Tawi certificate, the cash that leaves the bank and the posting — including gross-up when the payer bears the tax.

Book a USD invoice at one rate, settle it at another and watch the realized exchange gain or loss appear — or let the market wander for 30 days and see it tick.

One payment, several profit centers: document splitting creates one WHT line per profit center, and a tax report needs one row per line with the base prorated so the totals still tie to what was filed.

How I work

i.
SDLC Foundation
Waterfall + Iterative

Projects on XeerSoft followed a hybrid model: Waterfall for upfront discovery and FD authoring, then iterative UAT cycles with weekly Dev builds. Each project averaged 5 build cycles before Go-Live.

ii.
Agile Awareness
Sprint-like Cadence

Dev team delivered weekly builds. I tested 1–3 days per build (depending on complexity), met 2× per week for review and defect triage, functionally identical to Agile Sprint cadence with shorter cycles.

iii.
Documentation First
FD → ERD → UAT

Every project starts with a written FD before code. ERDs map data relationships. UAT test cases trace back to FD requirements. No code is built without a signed-off spec.
