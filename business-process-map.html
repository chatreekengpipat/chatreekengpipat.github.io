Business Process Master List

Before any config, a functional consultant maps the business processes the system has to run. This is a Business Process Master List (BPML) for the three end-to-end cycles every ERP turns on — Record-to-Report, Order-to-Cash and Procure-to-Pay — decomposed from the cycle (L1) down to the individual process (L3), each mapped to its T-codes, owning role, SAP module and a KPI. ABOUT THIS PROCESS MAP

This BPML is a methodology reference I authored. The three cycles and their process decomposition are the standard way SAP structures a finance/logistics landscape; the T-codes, roles and modules are the genuine mechanism. Each cycle is tied to a system where I delivered the equivalent end-to-end flow on XeerSoft Cloud ERP — the process thinking is real, the SAP framing is how I’d carry it onto an SAP project.
