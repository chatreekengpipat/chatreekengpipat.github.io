# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Two audiences land on the same surface, both deciding whether to trust Chatree
Kengpipat (Cee) with serious work:

- **Recruiters / hiring managers** evaluating an ERP Implementation Consultant.
  They skim fast, in a browser tab between other candidates, and want proof of
  real end-to-end delivery (configuration, master data, UAT, Go-live) and
  business-analyst capability (FSD, Use Case, User Story).
- **Prospective clients / companies** scoping an ERP partner or specialist.
  They arrive with a problem (a stalled rollout, a migration, a process gap) and
  want evidence this person has shipped the same kind of work on live projects.

The job both are trying to get done: in under a minute, answer "is this person
credible, and what specifically has he delivered?" — then find a fast path to
the project detail or to contact.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for Chatree Kengpipat, ERP Implementation Consultant on
XeerSoft Cloud ERP with prior SAP procurement experience at Mitsubishi Motors
Thailand. The site exists to convert a cold visitor into a qualified
conversation by making the depth of real implementation work legible at a
glance: 4 live projects, the methodology behind them, the academic and award
record, and a clear contact path. Success is a recruiter or client who leaves
convinced of the track record and reaches out.

## Brand Personality

Modern, technical, bold. The voice of an engineer-turned-consultant: precise,
evidence-first, quietly confident. It shows the work (real project artifacts,
dashboards, methodology) rather than asserting competence with adjectives.
Technical register is an asset here, not a liability — the audience respects
specificity (module names, project phases, system names) over marketing gloss.
Bold means committed visual and typographic choices, not loud or noisy.

## Anti-references

- **Generic portfolio / AI-template look.** No interchangeable "creative dev"
  template, no off-the-shelf theme that could belong to any of a thousand
  people. If a stranger could guess this was AI-generated or bought as a
  template, it has failed.
- The standard AI-slop tells specifically to avoid: tiny tracked uppercase
  eyebrows above every section, numbered `01 / 02 / 03` section scaffolding,
  identical icon-heading-text card grids, gradient text, decorative
  glassmorphism, and the hero-metric template.
- No marketing buzzwords (streamline, empower, leverage, seamless,
  world-class). The credibility comes from specific ERP nouns and verbs.

## Design Principles

1. **Show the work, don't claim it.** Lead with concrete artifacts — live
   projects, dashboards, methodology, the award — over self-description.
2. **Specific beats impressive.** Real module names, project phases, and system
   names (XeerSoft, SAP, FSD, UAT, Go-live) build more trust than superlatives.
3. **Engineer's precision.** Tight alignment, deliberate spacing rhythm, and
   committed typographic hierarchy read as competence to a technical audience.
4. **Fast to credibility.** A skimming visitor reaches "credible + what he
   delivered" within a minute; every section earns the scroll.
5. **One identity, two doors.** The same page serves recruiters and clients;
   keep a clear path to both project detail and contact without splitting the
   brand.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. The site already ships a light/dark theme toggle; both
themes must hold contrast (body text ≥4.5:1, large text ≥3:1) in either mode.
Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for the animated tech background and any
scroll/reveal motion. Keep interactive targets keyboard-reachable with visible
focus states.
