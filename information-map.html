WHY THE INFORMATION MAP MATTERS

A capability map says what the business does; the information map says what it does it to. The two are a pair — every capability acts on information concepts, and every concept is owned by a capability. Getting this model right is what keeps a data-migration honest (you can only load a Customer once you agree what a customer is), what makes an integration contract precise (which system masters the GL Account?), and what lets a close reconcile (does every Billing Document post exactly one balanced Journal Entry?). It is the same discipline as an SAP migration object catalogue and an integration map, lifted to the conceptual level and cross-mapped to the capability model.
