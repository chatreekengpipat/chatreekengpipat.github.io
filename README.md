## About

Mechanical Engineering graduate (B.Eng, Chulalongkorn University — Honors) working as an
**ERP Implementation Consultant & Business Analyst** at Learn Balance Group / Newton Sixth
Form School — the sole ERP consultant across a 3-branch, ~200-user organisation.

The work spans the full implementation lifecycle — requirements & FSD, configuration,
master data, UAT, and Go-live — across procurement, inventory, pricing, accounts
receivable, loyalty/CRM, and finance.

Currently studying **SAP S/4HANA (Sourcing & Procurement)** as a step toward SAP
functional consulting.
