RICEFW Register

Configuration covers most of an SAP build; the gaps become RICEFW objects — Reports, Interfaces, Conversions, Enhancements, Forms and Workflows. This register consolidates the custom development scoped across the methodology pages here into one catalogue, each with its module, complexity and a planning effort estimate. Filter by type.

Complexity bands map to a planning effort (Low ≈ 3–5 pd, Medium ≈ 8–12 pd, High ≈ 14–18 pd). The total is a planning figure, not a quote — it exists to show how I’d size and sequence a development backlog.

ABOUT THIS REGISTER

These objects are drawn from the proposal designs across this site — the four re-scoped ERP systems, the worked FSD, the integration map and the migration plan. RICEFW classification, complexity and effort sizing are the real SAP mechanism; the objects are proposed designs, tied to systems I delivered on XeerSoft Cloud ERP. A methodology artifact, not a delivered SAP backlog.
