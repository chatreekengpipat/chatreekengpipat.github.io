00 · The role, in one line

You own the tax layer of SAP FI — config, test, and support.

An SAP FI consultant specializing in tax configures, tests, and supports the tax determination and reporting layer of Financial Accounting — primarily VAT (Tax on Sales/Purchases) and Extended Withholding Tax, plus the GL account determination and statutory reporting that sit on top of every posting. You translate an agreed tax design into working configuration; you do not set the law.

On this S/4HANA programme you sit between several people — each one a clear hand-off. Pick a counterpart to see what you exchange with them.

Your value is the join between the legal requirement (from the Tax SME) and the working system (configured by you, built by ABAP, used through MM/SD/FI).

01 · What you own

Seven responsibility pillars.

This is the working surface of the role. Expand a pillar (click, or focus + Enter) to see the concrete activities and the T-codes behind them.

Build the engine that calculates and posts tax: the Thai calculation procedure TAXTH, tax codes and their rates/types in FTXP, the account determination that routes each tax transaction key to a GL account in OB40, and the GL master settings in FS00. For withholding, define Extended WHT types and codes in SPRO and link them to master data.

Make sure the right tax lands on the right document. Tax flows in from MM (the tax code on a purchase order / invoice verification), SD (output tax via condition records), and direct FI postings (FB60 vendor invoice, FB70 customer invoice). You verify input vs. output VAT splits and that WHT is withheld at payment.

FB60FB70F-53 MM tax codeSD MWST

Produce what the Revenue Department wants to see. Output VAT − input VAT is filed monthly on PP30 (data from the VAT report, e.g. RFUMSV00); withholding is filed on PND 3 / 53 / 54 with WHT certificates. On S/4HANA this increasingly runs through DRC / Advanced Compliance Reporting — worth knowing by name.

PP30PND 3/53/54 RFUMSV00DRC / ACR

The config only works if the master data carries the right tax attributes: tax classification on customer / vendor / material, the Extended WHT type & code assigned on the vendor master, VAT registration numbers, and the tax category on the GL master. You define the rules; data migration loads the volume.

Vendor · WHT infoCustomer · tax class. FS00 · tax category

Prove the tax behaves. Unit-test each tax code, integration-test the end-to-end flows that carry tax (P2P with input VAT + WHT, O2C with output VAT, period-end VAT/WHT runs), write and walk test scripts, and support UAT. Log and triage tax defects with clear reproduction steps — the same UAT discipline you ran on XeerSoft.

Get tax right at the switch. Validate open items that carry tax, opening tax balances, and any WHT carry-forward; confirm configuration transports moved correctly; and check the first live postings determine tax as designed. You support the tax slice of the cutover plan — you do not own the whole cutover.

Stabilise the first cycles. Support the first month-end VAT filing (PP30) and WHT filing (PND), reconcile the tax GL accounts, triage incidents on tax postings, and hand over clean knowledge-transfer docs so BAU / AMS can take it.

Petroleum-specific levies on the PTTEP client — Royalty, SRB, PITA — are not standard transactional tax codes. They land as GL provisions / period-end postings. Your job is to make sure the right GL accounts and postings exist — not to own the legal computation. (See the companion page for the petroleum-tax detail.)

03 · In / out of scope

The boundary is the scope.

Knowing what is not yours is as important as knowing what is. Toggle the view — what you deliver, what you explicitly hand off, and who is accountable for what.

▸ In scope — you deliver

  • Configure VAT tax codes (FTXP) & account determination (OB40) to the agreed design
  • Maintain Extended WHT types/codes and link to master data
  • Set tax-relevant GL master (FS00) attributes
  • Validate tax determination on MM, SD & FI documents
  • Prepare & execute tax test scripts; support UAT
  • Author functional specs for tax forms (WHT cert, PND, VAT layout)
  • Support tax data cutover & the first live filings

✕ Out of scope — hand off

  • Legal interpretation & rate-setting→ Client Tax SME / tax advisor
  • Re-designing the overall tax solution (fixed in Explore)→ FI/CO Lead + Tax SME
  • ABAP build of enhancements & form output→ Technical team (you write the spec)
  • Petroleum tax (Royalty / SRB / PITA) computation logic→ specialist provisioning; you ensure the GL exists
  • Corporate income-tax provisioning beyond config→ client finance / CO
  • Non-tax FI areas (treasury, AA policy) unless assigned→ respective FI streams

The honest one-liner: you implement an agreed tax design and prove it works — you don't author the tax law or build the ABAP.

09 · Legal watch-list

Thai tax law to respect when you configure or test.

The statutes and rules that turn into config decisions and test cases for PTTEP. Expand each — what it governs and the SAP implication to watch.

Governs
Petroleum income tax (20% PSC / 50% concession); interest non-deductible; 10-year loss carry-forward; USD functional currency; export "tax reference" price.
SAP watch
Model as a period-end GL provision & currency config — never as a transactional FTXP code.
Governs
Royalty (5–15%, PSC 10%) to DMF; SRB (0–75%, Thailand III only); concession vs PSC regimes.
SAP watch
GL provisioning + DMF remittance postings; ensure deductibility flags feed the PITA base correctly.
Governs
7% VAT, the tax point (time of supply), and the mandatory full tax-invoice fields (§86/4).
SAP watch
FTXP rate, output/input determination, tax-invoice form layout & number ranges.
Governs
Withholding on domestic & cross-border payments; certificate issuance; PND 3 / 53 / 54.
SAP watch
Extended WHT types/codes per income type; certificate & PND output programs.
Governs
The statutory 10% VAT reduced to 7%, renewed periodically by Royal Decree.
SAP watch
Maintain the tax-code rate with validity dates; plan for a rate-change cutover if the decree lapses.
Governs
Self-assessed VAT on services paid to overseas suppliers but used in Thailand.
SAP watch
Dedicated self-assessment tax code + PP36 reporting — critical for E&P importing rig / technical services.
Governs
PP30 (VAT) by the 15th of the next month (23rd via e-filing); PND by the 7th (extended for e-WHT).
SAP watch
Posting periods, the tax reporting date, and report-run timing must line up with these dates.
Governs
Revenue Department electronic tax invoice / receipt and e-WHT submission.
SAP watch
DRC / integration, digital signature, document numbering & XML output formats.
Governs
Arm's-length pricing for related parties (PTT group) and the petroleum export reference price.
SAP watch
Intercompany pricing conditions & documentation extracts to support the TP Disclosure Form.
Governs
Reduced / zero WHT on cross-border payments to treaty-country vendors holding a residence certificate.
SAP watch
A WHT code per treaty rate (PND 54) and a control to require the residence certificate before applying it.

Study reference only — not legal or tax advice. Always confirm with the client Tax SME / advisor and current Revenue Department / DMF guidance before configuration.
